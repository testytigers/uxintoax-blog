## The Short Answer

Heatmaps show you what users do. Not what they think.

Combine them with research to understand why.

---

## Types of Heatmaps

### Click Maps
Show where users click/tap. Hot spots = high engagement.

### Scroll Maps
Show how far users scroll. The fold is no longer real, but attention drops with distance.

### Move Maps
Show mouse movement. Correlates with eye movement (not perfectly).

### Attention Maps
AI-predicted attention based on scroll position and page structure.

---

## What Heatmaps Reveal

### Good Patterns
- Users clicking primary CTA
- Expected scroll depth
- Clear visual hierarchy

### Warning Signs
- Clicking non-clickable elements
- Ignoring key content
- Short scroll depth (on long pages)
- Rage clicks (repeated clicks on same spot)

---

## The Bottom Line

Heatmaps are a starting point, not a conclusion. They show what, not why. Pair with user interviews, session recordings, and analytics for the full picture.
