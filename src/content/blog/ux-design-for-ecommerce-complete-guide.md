## The Short Answer

E-commerce UX is simple in theory: make buying easy.

Harder than it sounds. Every extra click, every confusing label, every hidden cost costs you revenue.

---

## The 5 Critical Pages

### 1. Homepage
- Clear value proposition
- Featured products
- Easy navigation
- Trust signals

### 2. Product Listing
- Good filtering and sorting
- High-quality images
- Clear pricing
- Reviews and ratings

### 3. Product Page
- Detailed descriptions
- Multiple images/video
- Clear CTA
- Related products

### 4. Cart
- Editable quantities
- Visible totals
- Guest checkout option
- Trust badges

### 5. Checkout
- One page if possible
- Progress indicator
- No distractions
- Multiple payment options

---

## Conversion Tactics

### Show Social Proof
Reviews, ratings, "X people viewing this."

### Reduce Friction
Guest checkout. Auto-fill addresses. One-click payment.

### Build Trust
SSL badges. Clear return policy. Real customer photos.

### Create Urgency
"Only 3 left." "Sale ends soon." But be honest.

### Optimize for Mobile
Thumb-friendly buttons. Large tap targets. Simple navigation.

---

## The E-commerce UX Checklist

- [ ] Load time under 3 seconds
- [ ] Mobile responsive design
- [ ] Clear product images
- [ ] Transparent pricing (no hidden fees)
- [ ] Guest checkout available
- [ ] Multiple payment options
- [ ] Easy returns displayed
- [ ] Search with autocomplete
- [ ] Filter and sort options
- [ ] Reviews and ratings visible

---

## The Bottom Line

E-commerce UX is about trust and friction reduction. Make buying the easiest thing on your site. Be transparent. Be helpful. Remove every obstacle between interest and purchase.
