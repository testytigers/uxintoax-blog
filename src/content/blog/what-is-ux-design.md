## The Short Answer

UX design is the art of making things work well for the people who use them.

It's not about making things pretty (that's UI design). It's about making things work.

---

## What Is UX Design?

UX (User Experience) design is the process of designing digital products that solve real user problems.

It's not just interfaces. It's the entire experience — from discovering your product to using it to getting help.

### The 5 Stages of UX Design

1. **Empathize** — Understand users through research
2. **Define** — Frame the problem clearly
3. **Ideate** — Generate creative solutions
4. **Prototype** — Build testable versions
5. **Test** — Validate with real users

---

## UX vs UI Design

| UX Design | UI Design |
|-----------|-----------|
| How it works | How it looks |
| Research, structure, flow | Colors, typography, spacing |
| Wireframes and prototypes | Visual designs and mockups |
| "Does it make sense?" | "Does it look good?" |

They work together. UX is the skeleton. UI is the skin.

---

## Why UX Matters

- **Better products** — Solves real problems
- **More users** — People recommend products that work
- **Less churn** — Happy users stay
- **Less support** — Intuitive products need less help
- **Revenue** — Every dollar invested in UX returns $100

---

## The Bottom Line

UX design is about people. It's not about making things pretty. It's about making things work. Good UX is invisible — users don't notice it because it just... works.
