## The Short Answer

A UX case study isn't a portfolio of pretty screens.

It's proof that you can solve problems.

---

## The Case Study Structure

### 1. Project Overview (30 seconds)
- Client/Company
- Your role
- Timeline
- Tools used

### 2. Problem Statement (1 minute)
- What was broken?
- Why did it matter?
- What were the stakes?

### 3. Research & Discovery (2 minutes)
- User research methods
- Key insights
- Personas
- User journeys

### 4. Design Process (2 minutes)
- Wireframes
- Iterations
- Decisions and rationale
- User testing

### 5. Final Solution (1 minute)
- Screenshots
- Key features
- Design highlights

### 6. Results & Impact (30 seconds)
- Metrics improved
- User feedback
- Business impact
- Learnings

---

## What NOT to Include

- Every single screen
- Internal notes
- Failed experiments (unless they taught something)
- Client names (if NDA)
- Raw research data

---

## The Case Study Checklist

- [ ] Clear project context
- [ ] Specific problem statement
- [ ] Research evidence
- [ ] Design iterations shown
- [ ] Final solution with screenshots
- [ ] Quantified results
- [ ] Lessons learned
- [ ] Clean, skimmable layout
- [ ] Professional photos/diagrams

---

## The Bottom Line

Case studies sell your ability to solve problems. Show your process. Quantify results. Tell a story. Make it skimmable. One great case study beats ten mediocre ones.
