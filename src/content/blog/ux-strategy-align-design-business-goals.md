faq:
  - q: "What is UX strategy?"
    a: "UX strategy is the plan that aligns user experience design with business goals. It defines what kind of experience your product should provide, how that supports business objectives, and what steps to take to get there. It's not a design document — it's a business document that happens to be about users."
  - q: "How do I create a UX strategy?"
    a: "Start with business goals (revenue, retention, growth). Identify the user experiences that support those goals. Define the gap between current and desired experience. Create a roadmap with prioritized initiatives. Measure progress with UX metrics tied to business outcomes."
  - q: "How do UX strategy and business strategy differ?"
    a: "Business strategy sets goals (increase revenue, reduce costs). UX strategy defines the user experiences needed to achieve those goals. They're complementary — business strategy says what, UX strategy says how (from a user perspective)."

## The Short Answer

UX strategy bridges user needs and business goals.

It's what separates tactical designers from strategic leaders.

---

## The UX Strategy Framework

### 1. Business Goals
Revenue, retention, growth, efficiency. What is the business trying to achieve?

### 2. User Needs
Pain points, tasks, goals. What do users need to accomplish?

### 3. Experience Vision
The intersection. What kind of experience would satisfy both?

### 4. Roadmap
Prioritized initiatives. Timeline. Resources needed.

### 5. Metrics
How do we measure success? Tie UX metrics to business outcomes.

---

## Aligning UX with Business

### Revenue → Better Checkout Flow
### Retention → Better Onboarding
### Growth → Better Referral Experience
### Efficiency → Better Internal Tools
### Cost Reduction → Fewer Support Tickets

---

## Building Your UX Strategy

### Know the Business
- Revenue model
- Key metrics
- Competitive landscape
- Growth targets

### Know the Users
- Personas
- Pain points
- User journeys
- Research insights

### Find the Intersection
Where user needs and business goals overlap is your strategy.

---

## The Bottom Line

UX strategy isn't optional. It's what makes design a business function. Align your work with revenue, retention, and growth. Measure your impact. Become indispensable.
