    a: "Voice UX is designing conversational interfaces where users interact through speech rather than touch. Unlike screen interfaces, voice UIs are stateless, linear, and multi-modal. Users can only do one thing at a time, can't see a menu of options, and need clear audio feedback."
  - q: "How is voice design different from screen design?"
    a: "Voice design is linear (not hierarchical), stateless (each interaction starts fresh), auditory (not visual), and time-bound (users can't re-read a message). You design conversations, not screens. Error handling is harder because users can't scroll back."

    a: "Keep it brief (7-second rule), use natural language, provide clear confirmation, handle errors gracefully, design for interruptions, and always give users an escape path. Test with real users in real environments."

## The Short Answer

Voice UX is a different medium entirely.

No screens. No menus. Just conversation.

---

## The 5 Principles of Voice UX

### 1. Be Brief
Users remember 5-7 words. Keep responses short.

### 2. Be Natural
Speak like a person. Not like a machine.

### 3. Be Predictable
Same phrasing every time. Consistent patterns.

### 4. Be Helpful
Guide users. Anticipate their needs.

### 5. Be Forgiving
Users misspeak. Handle errors gracefully.

---

## Designing Voice Conversations

### Wake Words
- Clear and consistent
- Easy to say
- Not too common

### Responses
- 7-word rule
- Active voice
- Confirm actions
- Provide context

### Error Handling
- Acknowledge the error
- Explain what went wrong
- Offer a solution
- Stay positive

---

## Voice UX Checklist

- [ ] Brief responses (under 7 words)
- [ ] Natural language
- [ ] Clear confirmation
- [ ] Error recovery paths
- [ ] Graceful degradation
- [ ] Tested in noisy environments
- [ ] Multiple input patterns
- [ ] Privacy considerations
- [ ] Accessibility options
- [ ] Consistent tone

---

## The Bottom Line

Voice UX is about conversation, not screens. Design for the ear, not the eye. Be brief, be natural, be helpful. And never, ever make the user repeat themselves.
