## The Short Answer

Healthcare UX isn't like other UX. People's health is at stake.

One confusing button could delay treatment. One unclear label could cause medication errors.

---

## Who Uses Healthcare Apps?

### Patients
- Varying ages and tech literacy
- Often stressed or anxious
- Need simple, clear interfaces

### Healthcare Providers
- Time-pressed professionals
- Need quick, accurate information
- Work in high-pressure environments

### Administrators
- Manage scheduling, billing, records
- Need efficient workflows
- Handle large volumes of data

---

## The 5 Golden Rules

### 1. Safety First
No ambiguous actions. Confirm critical decisions. Show clear warnings.

### 2. Clear Over Clever
Plain language. No jargon. If a patient can't understand it, it's a risk.

### 3. Accessibility Is Mandatory
WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Screen reader support. Large text options.

### 4. Privacy Everywhere
Clear data usage explanations. Easy-to-find privacy controls. Transparent consent.

### 5. Respect Emotional States
Users may be scared, stressed, or overwhelmed. Be empathetic and patient.

---

## Designing for Clinical Workflows

### Medication Reminders
- Clear dosage instructions
- Time-specific notifications
- Confirmation before sending

### Appointment Scheduling
- Real-time availability
- Easy rescheduling
- Calendar integration
- Reminder notifications

### Patient Records
- Clear data organization
- Easy search and filter
- Secure access
- Export options

---

## Healthcare UX Checklist

- [ ] WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- [ ] HIPAA/GDPR compliant
- [ ] Clear error messages with actions
- [ ] No ambiguous buttons
- [ ] Large text and high contrast options
- [ ] Screen reader tested
- [ ] Emergency access clearly marked
- [ ] Data privacy transparent
- [ ] Tested with real patients
- [ ] Offline mode for critical features

---

## The Bottom Line

Healthcare UX saves lives. Design for clarity, safety, and empathy. Test with real users. Assume nothing. Every design decision is a patient safety decision.
