## The Short Answer

UX consulting is one of the highest-paying freelance paths.

But it's not just about great design. It's about great business.

---

## Pricing Models

### Hourly
$75-$200+/hour
- Simple to calculate
- Penalizes efficiency
- Risk of scope creep

### Project-Based
$5,000-$50,000+ per project
- Values your expertise
- Clear deliverables
- Recommended

### Retainer
$3,000-$15,000/month
- Predictable income
- Long-term relationship
- Best for growth

---

## Finding Clients

### Inbound
- Portfolio website
- LinkedIn presence
- Blog writing
- Speaking at events

### Outbound
- Cold outreach with specific value
- Referrals from past clients
- Agency partnerships
- LinkedIn networking

---

## The Consulting Process

### 1. Discovery Call
Understand the problem. Scope the work.

### 2. Proposal
Clear deliverables, timeline, and pricing.

### 3. Agreement
Signed contract. Scope, timeline, payment terms.

### 4. Execution
Research, design, test, deliver.

### 5. Handoff
Documentation, training, support plan.

---

## The Bottom Line

UX consulting is design + business. Get good at both. Price your value, not your time. Build relationships, not just deliverables.
