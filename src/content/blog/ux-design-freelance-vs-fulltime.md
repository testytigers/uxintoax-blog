## The Short Answer

Startup UX is different from big tech UX in one word: **constraints.**

You have less time, less budget, less team, and more responsibility. That's not a bad thing — it's what makes startup UX exciting.

---

## How Startup UX Differs

| Big Tech | Startup |
|----------|---------|
| Specialized roles | Wear multiple hats |
| 6-month design cycles | Ship in days |
| Large budgets | Bootstrap budgets |
| Many stakeholders | Fewer, direct conversations |
| Process-heavy | Process-light |
| Safe, incremental changes | Bold, risky bets |

---

## The Startup UX Playbook

### 1. Design Lean
- Sketch before Figma
- Prototype before design
- Test before build
- Ship before perfect

### 2. Talk to Users Weekly
You don't need a research department. You need 5 conversations per week.

### 3. Focus on the One Thing
Startups have one chance to prove product-market fit. Design for that one thing. Ignore the rest.

### 4. Iterate Fast
Ship. Learn. Iterate. Repeat. Your first design will be wrong. Your 10th will be right.

### 5. Wear Multiple Hats
You'll do research, design, some prototyping, maybe even some QA. Embrace it.

---

## Common Startup UX Mistakes

- **Over-designing** — Ship the MVP first
- **No user feedback** — Talk to users, not just founders
- **Building features, not solving problems** — Focus on outcomes, not outputs
- **Ignoring data** — Track metrics from day one
- **Copying competitors** — Solve your users' problems, not theirs

---

## The Bottom Line

Startup UX is messy, fast, and incredibly rewarding. You'll see the impact of your work in days, not months. Embrace constraints. Ship fast. Learn faster.
