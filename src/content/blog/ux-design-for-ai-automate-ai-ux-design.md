## The Short Answer

AI isn't replacing UX designers.

It's replacing the boring parts of UX design.

---

## How AI Is Changing UX

### Research
- Automated sentiment analysis
- Behavioral pattern detection
- Survey response clustering
- Interview transcription + insights

### Design
- Layout suggestions
- Auto-generated wireframes
- Design system optimization
- Color palette generation

### Prototyping
- Text-to-UI generation
- Auto-layout
- Component suggestions
- Responsive auto-sizing

### Testing
- Automated usability scoring
- A/B test prediction
- Conversion probability
- Accessibility scanning

---

## AI Tools Every UX Designer Should Know

### Research

- Otter.ai (transcription)
- Typeform AI (survey analysis)

### Design
- Uizard (text-to-design)
- Galileo AI (prototype generation)
- Relume (wireframe generation)

### Prototyping
- Figma AI
- Adobe Firefly
- Anima AI

### Testing
- UserTesting AI
- Hotjar AI insights
- VWO AI optimization

---

## The Skills That Matter More in the AI Era

### AI Can Do
- Layout generation
- Pattern detection
- Content optimization
- Repetitive tasks

### You Must Do
- Empathy and understanding
- Strategic thinking
- Stakeholder alignment
- Creative problem-solving

---

## The Bottom Line

AI is a tool, not a replacement. The designers who thrive will be the ones who use AI to automate the boring stuff and focus on what humans do best: understanding people.
