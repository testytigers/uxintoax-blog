## The Short Answer

In 2025, **Figma** dominates UX design. But it's not the only tool you need.

Great UX design requires a toolkit: research tools, design software, prototyping platforms, and collaboration apps. You don't need all of them — pick the ones that fit your workflow.

Here are the tools that matter, ranked by importance.

---

## The Essentials (Start Here)

### 1. Figma — The Industry Standard

If you learn one tool, make it Figma. It does everything: design, prototype, collaborate, and hand off to developers.

**What it does best:**
- Real-time collaborative design
- Component-based design systems
- Interactive prototyping
- Dev mode for developer handoff
- Auto Layout for responsive design

**Pricing:** Free plan available. Pro: $12/month. Organization: $52/month.

**Who it's for:** Everyone from beginners to enterprise teams.

**Why it wins:** The collaboration model changed everything. Multiple people can work on the same file simultaneously. Comments, version history, and dev handoff are built-in.

### 2. Miro — Collaboration Whiteboard

Miro is your digital workshop. Use it for brainstorming, user journey mapping, affinity diagramming, and stakeholder workshops.

**What it does best:**
- Visual collaboration in real-time
- Pre-built templates (journey maps, canvases, matrices)
- Sticky note organization
- Integration with Figma
- Infinite canvas for large-scale projects

**Pricing:** Free plan. Teams: $8/month per person.

**Who it's for:** UX researchers, product managers, designers, and any team that needs visual collaboration.

---

## Research Tools

### 3. Maze — Remote Usability Testing

Test your Figma prototypes with real users without scheduling sessions.

**What it does best:**
- Upload Figma prototypes directly
- Automated task completion metrics
- Heatmaps and attention tracking
- Unmoderated testing with large sample sizes
- AI-powered insights

**Pricing:** Free plan. Pro: $55/month.

**Who it's for:** UX designers who need to validate designs with users at scale.

### 4. Hotjar — User Behavior Analytics

See how real users interact with your live product through heatmaps, session recordings, and feedback polls.

**What it does best:**
- Heatmaps showing click and scroll patterns
- Session recordings of real user behavior
- Feedback polls and surveys embedded in pages
- Funnel analysis for conversion tracking

**Pricing:** Free plan (1000 pageviews/month). Plus: €39/month.

**Who it's for:** UX designers working on live products who need to understand actual user behavior.

### 5. Otter.ai — Meeting Transcription

Never lose a user interview insight. Otter records and transcribes interviews in real-time.

**What it does best:**
- Real-time transcription during interviews
- Searchable transcripts
- AI-powered summaries and highlights
- Speaker identification
- Figma integration

**Pricing:** Free plan. Pro: $8.33/month.

---

## Prototyping & Interaction Design

### 6. ProtoPie — Advanced Prototyping

Figma's prototypes are great, but ProtoPie handles complex interactions, sensors, and conditional logic.

**What it does best:**
- Sensor-based interactions (gyroscope, camera)
- Conditional logic and variables
- Cross-platform prototyping (iOS, Android, web, IoT)
- Smart components
- Real device testing

**Pricing:** Free plan. Plus: $12/month.

**Who it's for:** Designers building complex prototypes for hardware, mobile apps, or IoT products.

### 7. Framer — Design to Website

Build production websites directly from your designs. Framer blurs the line between design and development.

**What it does best:**
- Design websites without code
- AI-powered design generation
- CMS for content management
- Built-in hosting and analytics
- Interactive prototypes that are also real websites

**Pricing:** Free plan. Pro: $15/month.

**Who it's for:** Designers who want to ship real products, not just prototypes.

---

## Specialized Tools

### 8. Optimal Workshop — Information Architecture

Card sorting, tree testing, and site navigation research. Essential for complex products with lots of content.

**What it does best:**
- Card sorting to understand user mental models
- Tree testing to validate navigation
- First Click Testing for task design
- Regression testing for IA changes

**Pricing:** Free trials available. Suite: $290/year.

**Who it's for:** UX designers working on complex websites and apps with information architecture challenges.

### 9. UserTesting — Moderated User Research

Professional moderated and unmoderated testing with built-in recruitment and analytics.

**What it does best:**
- Recruit real users for testing
- Moderated sessions with live feedback
- AI-powered insights from test recordings
- Benchmarking against competitors
- Integration with Figma and Miro

**Pricing:** Starts at $49/test.

**Who it's for:** Teams that need high-quality, recruited user testing without the hassle of finding participants.

### 10. Whimsical — Wireframing Made Simple

Fast, simple wireframing and flowcharts. Perfect for early-stage ideation and quick stakeholder communication.

**What it does best:**
- Lightning-fast wireframing
- Flowcharts and mind maps
- Sticky notes and diagrams
- Real-time collaboration
- Simple, intuitive interface

**Pricing:** Free plan. Pro: $10/month.

**Who it's for:** Designers and product managers who need to communicate ideas quickly without getting bogged down in pixel-perfect design.

---

## Design Systems & Handoff

### 11. Zeroheight — Design System Documentation

Beautiful, developer-friendly documentation for your design system. Bridges the gap between design and engineering.

**What it does best:**
- Auto-syncs with Figma components
- Code snippets for developers
- Usage guidelines and best practices
- Version history and changelog
- Custom branding and organization

**Pricing:** Free plan. Team: $30/month.

**Who it's for:** Teams with design systems who need to keep designers and developers aligned.

### 12. Storybook — Component Development Environment

Develop, test, and document UI components in isolation. The gold standard for component-driven development.

**What it does best:**
- Isolated component development
- Interactive documentation
- Visual testing and regression detection
- Addon ecosystem (a11y, storysource, knobs)
- Integration with design tokens

**Pricing:** Free and open source.

**Who it's for:** Engineering teams building component libraries with design system support.

## AI-Powered Tools (The New Wave)

### 13. Galileo AI — AI Design Assistant

Generate UI designs from text descriptions. Not a replacement for designers — a starting point.

**What it does best:**
- Generate Figma designs from text prompts
- Mobile and web interface generation
- Rapid exploration of design options
- Learning from your design system

**Pricing:** Pro: $20/month.

**Who it's for:** Designers who want to explore concepts faster and iterate on ideas before deep diving.

### 14. Uizard — AI Design Tool

Turn sketches and text prompts into editable designs. Great for non-designers and rapid prototyping.

**What it does best:**
- Text-to-design generation
- Sketch-to-design conversion
- Auto-styling and theme generation
- Team collaboration features
- AI-powered design suggestions

**Pricing:** Free plan. Pro: $29/month.

**Who it's for:** Product managers, founders, and designers who want rapid prototyping from sketches or ideas.

### 15. Magician — Figma Plugin for AI

AI-powered icons, images, and text generation inside Figma. The most useful AI tool currently integrated into your design workflow.

**What it does best:**
- Generate icons, images, and SVGs with AI
- Auto-generate placeholder text
- Create image variations
- Built directly into Figma

**Pricing:** Free plan. Pro: included in Figma Pro.

**Who it's for:** Figma users who want AI assistance without leaving their design environment.

## How to Choose Your Tools

### The Beginner Stack (Free)
- Figma (design + prototype)
- Google Forms (surveys)
- Miro (collaboration)
- Otter.ai (transcription)

### The Professional Stack
- Figma Pro
- Maze (usability testing)
- Hotjar (analytics)
- Optimal Workshop (IA research)
- Zeroheight (design system docs)

### The Enterprise Stack
- All of the above
- UserTesting (recruited research)
- Storybook (component library)
- Tableau or Mixpanel (data analytics)
- Custom integrations with Jira/Linear

## The Bottom Line

You don't need all 15 tools. Start with **Figma + Miro** — they cover 70% of UX work. Add research tools as your practice matures. Don't buy tools to solve workflow problems — identify the actual problem first, then find the tool that solves it.

The best tool is the one your team actually uses.
